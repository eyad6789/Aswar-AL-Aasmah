About Us

Iraq's Trusted Trading Partner for Quality Tools & Equipment

Aswar AlAsimah Company is a leading Iraqi general trading company headquartered in Baghdad. We specialize in importing and distributing high-quality electrical materials, industrial hand tools, wires, cables, and equipment from world-class manufacturers.

As a limited liability company, we serve contractors, electricians, industrial operations, and retailers across Iraq — providing everything from precision Cr-V pliers to complete electrical infrastructure solutions and home & office furniture.
